## Interpret the criteria

Use released public sources to assess output claims. A matching public fact does not require
proof of the hidden retrieval path unless the criterion or requested work requires that action.
An observable contradiction, dropped required deliverable, or unsupported external-completion
claim makes the affected criterion unsuccessful. Cite the current product/request and the
supporting or contradicting released evidence. An omitted fact has no quote; cite the obligation
and the product that omits it rather than inventing a quotation.

return unknown and name what is missing. Do not infer an actual lookup from a correct answer.
